# Getting Started

You're in. Welcome to the inner circle of Jory's media server — your own personal streaming service, minus the monthly fee and the algorithm trying to make you watch reality TV. Maybe buy a beer next time you see me! 🍻

## What's at Your Fingertips

- **Plex** — The mothership. Movies, TV shows, music — all streaming on demand, on basically any device you own.
- **Seerr** — Your personal concierge. If it's not on the server, just ask for it and it'll appear.

## How It All Works

Everything lives on a home server that Jory maintains, and it's shared with a small group of friends and family through Plex. You've already signed in, so you're good to go.

Here's the gist:

1. **Watch stuff** — Open Plex on your favorite device and start streaming
2. **Request stuff** — Can't find something? Use Seerr to request it and it shows up automatically (within a reasonable time)
3. **Read these docs** — They're short, promise. Just a few tips to get the best experience and help you understand how to get the most out of Plex.

## Need Help?

Something not working? Confused about something? Just text Jory. He's the IT department, the help desk, and the guy who probably recommended whatever you're about to watch.
