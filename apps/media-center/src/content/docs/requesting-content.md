# Requesting Content

Want to watch something that isn't on the server yet? You can request it through Seerr, and it'll be added automatically.

## How to Request

1. Open Seerr from the dashboard or navigate to it directly
2. Sign in with your Plex account
3. Search for the movie or TV show you want
4. Click **Request** and confirm

## What Happens Next

Once you submit a request:

- The request is automatically processed
- The content is downloaded and added to the Plex library
- You'll be able to find it in Plex once it's ready (usually within minutes for movies, and varies for TV shows)

## Tips

- **Movies** are usually available within 5–15 minutes of requesting
- **TV shows** can be requested by season — you can request individual seasons or the entire series
- If a show is currently airing, new episodes will be added automatically as they become available
- Check back on Seerr to see the status of your requests
