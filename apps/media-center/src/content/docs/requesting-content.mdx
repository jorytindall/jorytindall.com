# Requesting Content

Can't find that movie everyone's talking about? That show your coworker won't shut up about? No problem — you can request it, and the server will handle the rest.

## How to Request

1. Head to [**Seerr**](https://request.jorytindall.tv) from the dashboard.
2. Sign in with your Plex account
3. Search for whatever your heart desires
4. Hit **Request** and confirm (don't worry about quality, select "Any"; my services automate all of this)

That's it. Seriously.

## What Happens Behind the Scenes

Once you click that button, a chain of delightful automation kicks in:

- Your request is picked up instantly
- I personally approve or deny the download 😘
- The content gets downloaded and organized
- It lands in Plex, ready to stream
- If you request through Seerr, you'll get an email notification when the request is available

You don't need to do anything else. Go make some popcorn.

## Pro Tips

- **Movies** usually show up the quickest, sometimes even just a few minutes depending on the download queue.
- **TV shows** can be requested by season, so grab just what you need or go all in on the whole series
- Currently airing shows? New episodes get added automatically as they drop
- Check Seerr anytime to see where your requests stand
- **New Releases** might take a bit of time to show up on services that I scan 🏴‍☠️, so be patient. A new movie on Netflix can sometimes land in a day or two, or sometimes it can take a couple of weeks. It depends on the popularity generally.
