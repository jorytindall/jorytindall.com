# Plex Apps

Plex runs on just about everything — your phone, your TV, your tablet, probably your fridge if it has a screen. Here's where to grab it and how to dial in the settings.

## Where to Download

- **iOS / iPadOS** — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/plex/id383457673)
- **Android** — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.plexapp.android)
- **Apple TV** — Available in the App Store on your Apple TV (this is Jory's personal recommendation!)
- **Roku** — Available in the Roku Channel Store
- **Fire TV** — Available in the Amazon Appstore
- **Smart TVs** — Most Samsung, LG, and Vizio TVs have Plex built in (Smart TV apps are generally the worst experience)
- **Web** — Use [app.plex.tv](https://app.plex.tv) in any browser
- **Desktop** — Download from [plex.tv/media-server-downloads](https://www.plex.tv/media-server-downloads/#plex-app) (You likely do not need this)

## Settings That Actually Matter

A couple of quick tweaks will make everything look and sound way better:

### Video Quality
- **At home on Wi-Fi**: Crank it to **Maximum** or **Original**. This streams the full quality file directly — no compression, no compromises.
- **On the go**: Start with **720p 4 Mbps** and bump it up if your connection can handle it.

### Subtitles
- Set them to **Auto** and they'll only kick in when the audio is in a different language. No fiddling every time.

## Do I Need Plex Pass?

Nope. A free Plex account gets you full access to everything on the server. Plex Pass is only needed if you want extras like offline downloads on your phone — that's up to you.
